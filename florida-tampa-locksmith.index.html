Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

How fast can you get to me?

In most parts of Pinellas, Pasco, and Hillsborough counties, a technician can reach you quickly — call and we'll give you an estimated arrival time up front.

How much does a service call cost?

Pricing depends on the job — a simple lockout costs less than a full rekey or key programming. We'll quote you a price on the phone before dispatching.

Can you make a new car key if I've lost every copy?

Yes. We can cut and program a new key or fob on-site for most makes and models, even without the original key present.

Do you charge more at night or on weekends?

We aim to keep pricing consistent around the clock. Ask about current rates when you call — we're transparent about cost before we start.

Do you work on smart locks and push-to-start vehicles?

Yes, our technicians are equipped to handle modern smart locks and push-to-start key systems in addition to traditional locks and keys.
